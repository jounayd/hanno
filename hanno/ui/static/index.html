Miner settings

Saved to config/local.yaml (gitignored) as an overlay on top of the checked-in config/default.yaml — the documented defaults are never rewritten.

Hosted APIs finish well under the 600s default. Self-hosted local models are much slower tok/s -- raise this (e.g. 1800-3600) or long responses get killed mid-generation and logged as a failed candidate.

--until-promising: how many of this iteration's eligible candidates get Lean-checked, not just the single top-scoring one -- self-rated score alone isn't a trustworthy enough signal to gate the whole check budget down to one winner.
